### Added

- **Work page endpoints serve a project or any container's sections** (MMR-469). `GET /api/projects/:key/work` and `GET /api/nodes/:id/work` (an initiative or phase, covering its whole subtree) return what is running now, what needs you, the frontier of groomed and ungroomed work one step from startable, what shipped in the last seven days (counted in the `?tz=` zone you send), and what is going stale. A task id is refused.
