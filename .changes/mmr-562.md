### Added

- **The All projects sidebar lists your pinned projects and searches the rest** (MMR-562). On `/` and `/doctor` the sidebar now has a Projects section under Work: the pinned projects as links with their decision counts, or the five most recently active projects when none is pinned. Its search box filters every project in place, archived ones included, with the switcher's keys: the arrows move, Enter opens, and Alt+P pins. `/` focuses the search from anywhere on the page, and Escape clears it. A pinned project now opens with one click from the home page.
