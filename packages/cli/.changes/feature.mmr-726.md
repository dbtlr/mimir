- **Record pages share one header, with tabs on projects, initiatives, and phases** (MMR-726). A project, initiative, phase, or task page opens with a quiet trail of its ancestors (a phone folds the middle ones into "…"), the title, a status line that reads the status, then the type and ID, and the summary clamped to three lines with _more_. Beside it sits the one action the record's state calls for: _New task_ on a project, initiative, or phase; _Start_ on a ready task; _Approve_ and _Send back…_ on a task in review, where the review block no longer repeats them; and nothing while an agent runs a task or it waits on others. Everything else is under _More_, grouped as _Edit_ (with _Move…_ and _Set grooming…_), the status changes, the holds, then _Abandon…_ on its own. The _Launch in T3_ split button moves into _More_ as a one-click _Start on <System>_ row per T3 system, the default system first, then _Start in T3…_, which opens the launch settings; and a project's settings open from _More_ › _Edit_. Initiatives and phases trade their right rail and its _Direction_, _About_, and _Artifacts_ chips for _Work_, _Details_, _Timeline_, and _Artifacts_ tabs; a project has _Work_, _Tasks_, _Seeds_, _Artifacts_, and _Details_, its task, seed, and artifact lists now opening under its header. A tab with nothing in it is hidden, and the URL keeps the tab (`?tab=details`), so a reload or a shared link opens the same one. The project's findings chip becomes a _Record health_ row at the top of _Work_. A task keeps its body, its rail, and its _Links_, _Agent_, and _Details_ chips; its _Actions_ chip is gone.
