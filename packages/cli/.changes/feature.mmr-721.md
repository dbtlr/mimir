- **The Needs you deck is ready to mount** (MMR-721). It shows what needs you as one ranked deck of cards across projects: a stopped delivery with _Clear stop_, a delivery's or a project's provisional decisions in one card with _Review N decisions_, a question, led by _Question_, with _Answer…_ (MMR-752), and a task blocked on you. A phone shows one card at a time with the next one peeking, and a desktop shows pages of four. Each card names its project after its ID. After a decisions walkthrough opened from the deck, Close turns the deck back to that card and focuses its button, even when the card is on another page. The console's `/kit` page shows the deck over sample data; Home will mount it.
