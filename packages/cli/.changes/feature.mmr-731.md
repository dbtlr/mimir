- **A task's Edit opens one sheet for everything about it** (MMR-731). In the console, _Edit_ in a task's _More_ opens a sheet (full screen on a phone, a panel from the right on desktop) with the title, summary, and description (with _Write_ and _Preview_), then _Planning_ (size, priority, grooming while the task is to do, and its parent) and _Links_ (tags and the external ref). _Move…_ and _Set grooming…_ leave _More_: the parent and the grooming are fields of the sheet. Choosing _Needs answers_ asks for the open questions, saved as a note on the task; the other grooming states no longer take a note. _Save_ sends only what changed; when part of it fails, the sheet stays open on the field that didn't save, says what did, and a second _Save_ sends only what failed. A size, priority, summary, or external ref can now be cleared from the console.
