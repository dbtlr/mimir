- **The sidebar drawer closes with a swipe** (MMR-799). Below 1024 pixels wide, the sidebar's drawer now closes when you drag or flick it toward the left edge, as well as on Escape or a tap outside it. It also closes when you go Back between two tabs of one page or from an in-page link, where it used to stay open. It sits over the same dimmed, blurred backdrop as the console's other sheets. Its rows, its size, and Jump to behave as before.
