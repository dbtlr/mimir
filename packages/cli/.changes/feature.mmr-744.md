- **Notes and decision answers use one text box each, built to the console's design guidelines** (MMR-744). A task's note composer now starts one line tall and grows with its text to four lines, in the same 15px box as the console's forms, with _Append_ beside it at the same height. While a note saves, its text stays read-only and _Append_ holds. A saved note empties the box and returns focus to it for the next one. A refused note keeps its text and returns focus to _Append_.
- **The decisions walkthrough answers with arrow-key choices.** Its answers are now a radio group in the console's accent, so the arrow keys move the selection and the whole row, its detail included, takes a tap. While a _Send_ writes, the answers and notes stay readable but cannot change. Its text, buttons, and spacing follow the console's type and spacing scale: 44px controls, the 13px meta voice, and IDs that keep to one line in the header.
