- **Home is one feed column** (MMR-722). It opens with the day as its title and one sentence under it that says how many cards need you and how many items they hold, how many tasks are running, and how many shipped this week. Below it come the one-time notifications card when it applies, _Needs you_ as the ranked deck of cards, its head counting both ("10 cards, 26 items"), and _Now_ as title cards for the most recently started tasks, four on a phone and six on a desktop, its head reading "6 of 9" when more are running. _Shipped this week_ shows the seven-day chart and a count per project, and _When you have a minute_ counts seeds to triage, tasks ready to start, tasks going cold, and `doctor` findings. A section with nothing in it is left out, and when nothing needs you, one line says so. Tapping a _Now_ card opens its preview, as a deck card does. The right rail and its project health strip are gone, since the sidebar lists the projects. Home no longer lists seeds, runnable tasks, or going-cold tasks one by one; the seeds page and each project's work page still do. `/?mode=focus` is unchanged.
