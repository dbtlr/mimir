- **The console's sidebar lists containers** (MMR-730). On every page it shows a ← link up one level, the project, initiative, or phase you are in, and the initiatives or phases inside it, each with its status dot and done-over-total count (∞, _parked_, or _Empty_ where that fits). Finished ones fold into one line that closes again when you move on. On Home and other pages outside a project, the list is Home and up to six projects: those that need you first, then pinned, then recent, then **See all N projects**. A task's page outlines the phase or initiative that holds it. The Work, Tasks, Seeds, and Artifacts rows and the full outline are gone, since the project page's tabs reach those lists. The switcher's edge and chevron now match Jump to's.
