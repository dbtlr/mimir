- **The console reads at a glance on a phone** (MMR-718). No text that carries information is smaller than 12px, and none draws in the faint inks: IDs, timestamps, captions, counts, hints, and placeholders now use the dim ink, at 4.5:1 or better in both themes. Feed row and card titles are 16px at medium weight, so they lead their 13px meta lines, on phones and desktops alike. A status reads as its coloured dot and a bright semibold word, so _Parked_ and _Abandoned_ are as legible as _In progress_. A record header now leads with its status, then its type and ID. The rules are in ADR 0019's MMR-718 refinement and the console's design guidelines ([DESIGN.md](../host/DESIGN.md)).
