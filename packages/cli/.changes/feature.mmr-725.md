- **The Seeds and Artifacts tabs move onto the list layout, and Home's Seeds to triage row links into it** (MMR-725). A project's Seeds tab opens with a count line that leads with the open seeds and breaks them down by lane ("43 open seeds: 11 to triage, 2 ready to resolve, 15 promoted, 15 parked"), _File a seed_ beside it, a search field, and _Filter_. Seeds are cards grouped by lane that open a preview; Parked and Settled fold after the live lanes. _To triage_, _Ready to resolve_, _Parked_, and _Settled_ are each a `show` value in the URL with a removable chip, and the Filter sheet sets Show and any number of kinds (`kind=bug,feature`). The Artifacts tab lists artifacts newest first, folded by day with only the newest day open and five rows a day before _Show N more_; the list loads 50 at a time with _Show 50 more_ in place of loading on scroll. Its Filter sheet picks one kind, and a tag or date a link carries keeps its own removable chip. An artifact's kind now reads from a `kind:` tag, then from the bare tags `session_summary`, `spec`, `plan`, `mock`, `investigation`, `report`, `spike`, `audit`, and `verification`. On Home, _Seeds to triage_ opens that project's _To triage_ seeds when every seed to triage belongs to one project. The ⌘ glyph now draws from a system symbol font, since the console's fonts have none.
