- **Create and edit records in one Form sheet** (MMR-743). File a seed, New project, Project settings, and Launch in T3 now open as a form that fills the screen on a phone and slides in as a 640px panel from the right on a desktop. A pinned header names the form, and a pinned footer holds the commit, with Cancel beside it on a desktop. The form opens on its first field, keeps the commit disabled until the form can be sent, sends it once, and holds while it saves. Fields are larger and further apart, with 14px labels and 15px values. Help appears only for a field that behaves unexpectedly, such as the seed's _Shift+Enter_ line or the key's permanence. A scrolled form always rests with a field's label 24px below the header.
- **File a seed** keeps the `s` hotkey and Enter to file. Kind is now _Idea_, _Bug_, or _Feature_, and Project is picked by search. The button reads _File seed_.
- **New project and Project settings** are now one project form, and both label the name _Title_. Settings names the project's key under its title, and _Lifecycle_ with _Archive project_ is the last group of the form. Archiving still asks nothing: the Notice reads _Archived **Mimir**_ with _Undo_.
- **Launch in T3** names the task under its title and offers System, Project, Model, and Runtime as menus, with reasoning effort in the provider's own words (`low`, `medium`, `high`). Workspace, Branch, and _Plan first_ fold under _Advanced_, and Branch shows only for a new worktree. Closing the form on a desktop now returns focus to the control that opened it instead of the page.
- **Find records with one picker**: the jump palette and the project switcher share the Record picker's look, and a form's project field opens a searchable list.
- **Notices sit on the page's gutter**: 8px below the top bar and 14px from the sides on a phone, and on the content column's 20px gutter on a desktop. A Notice now has an edge brighter than a card's and a lighter shadow in the light theme, and its action is a text button such as _Undo_.
- **Another browser's notification kinds** open in the same sheet as the console's other content: from the bottom on a phone and as a 440px panel on a desktop.
