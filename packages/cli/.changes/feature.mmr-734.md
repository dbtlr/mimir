- **The review page suggests turning on notifications, once** (MMR-734). A browser that can receive Web Push and has not been asked for permission sees a card above Needs you, "Get notified when something needs you", with Turn on and Not now, when the host's push channel is ready. Either answer retires the card in that browser, and so does a Turn on from Settings › Notifications. If Turn on does not work, the card says why and links to Settings › Notifications.
