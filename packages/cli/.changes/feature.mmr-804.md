- **A project's sidebar shows what needs you there** (MMR-804). On every page of a project, the console's sidebar holds a _Needs you_ slot under the switcher. Its head counts the project's Needs you cards. Up to three rows follow in the deck's order, each the card's word (_Question_, _Stopped_, _Decisions_, or _Blocked_) and its title, and each opens where the card's Open leads. _See all N_ opens the project's Work tab when more cards wait. A project with nothing waiting says "Nothing needs you in" and its name. Home, Settings, and archived projects have no slot. See [ADR 0013](../../docs/decisions/0013-ui-embedded-spa.md).
- **The phone drawer clears the top inset** (MMR-804). On a phone with a notch or status bar, the sidebar drawer's first row no longer sits under it.
