- **Menus open as a bottom sheet on a phone, and notices share one look** (MMR-742). The top bar's _More_ menu, the T3 launch caret, and another browser's actions on Settings › Notifications now open as a bottom sheet below the tablet width, with rows 44 pixels tall and a _Cancel_ row, and as a menu anchored to the button from there up. The theme in _More_ is now a pair of choices, _Dark_ and _Light_, and picking one leaves the menu open. On Settings › Notifications, another browser's row shows its _•••_ button from the tablet width up, where its menu opens anchored to it. _Remove this browser_ there now asks first, in a centred confirm that opens on _Cancel_, because removing another browser cannot be undone. After _Send test notification_ from a phone's row sheet, focus returns to the row. The archive toast now offers _Undo_ where it said _Unarchive_. Every toast, with its _Retry_ or _Undo_ button, and the offline and update banners go through one notice component.
