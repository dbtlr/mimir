mimir resolve · Resolve a seed: what it asked for is done. The reason is recorded.

USAGE
  mimir resolve <id> [reason...] [options]

ARGUMENTS
  id      The seed, KEY-sN.
  reason  Why, in the rest of the line. Required, but may follow --.

OPTIONS
      --ascii            Print without color, in ASCII glyphs.
  -f, --format <format>  Select the output format, records by default. One of: ids, json, jsonl, records, table.

GLOBAL OPTIONS
  -h, --help  Show this help.

EXAMPLES
  $ mimir resolve MMR-s3 shipped in MMR-40
    the reason is the rest of the line
