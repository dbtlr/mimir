mimir park · Put a task on hold. Also takes a seed (KEY-sN). Also takes a delivery (KEY-dN): hold none → paused, with a required reason.

USAGE
  mimir park <id> [reason...] [options]

ARGUMENTS
  id      The task (KEY-seq), the seed (KEY-sN), or the delivery (KEY-dN).
  reason  For a task or a seed the reason is optional and recorded in the transition log; for a delivery it is required and recorded in the delivery history.

OPTIONS
      --ascii            Print without color, in ASCII glyphs.
  -f, --format <format>  Select the output format, records by default. One of: ids, json, jsonl, records, table.
      --fields <fields>  Select top-level fields of json and jsonl output, as a comma list.  (repeatable)

GLOBAL OPTIONS
  -h, --help      Show this help.
      --no-input  Ask no questions, and take every answer from options.  (default: true)

EXAMPLES
  $ mimir park MMR-3 "waiting on design"
    put it on hold
  $ mimir park MMR-s4 "after v1"
    a seed: kept, no longer untriaged
