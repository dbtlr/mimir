mimir item drop · Drop an open item with a reason: it will not be done, for good.

USAGE
  mimir item drop <task> <number> [words...] [options]

ARGUMENTS
  task    The task (KEY-seq).
  number  The item number.
  words   Why it is dropped, the rest of the line.

OPTIONS
      --reason <text>    Why it is dropped, in place of the words after the number.
      --ascii            Print without color, in ASCII glyphs.
  -f, --format <format>  Select the output format, records by default. One of: ids, json, jsonl, records, table.
      --fields <fields>  Select top-level fields of json and jsonl output, as a comma list.  (repeatable)

GLOBAL OPTIONS
  -h, --help      Show this help.
      --no-input  Ask no questions, and take every answer from options.  (default: true)

EXAMPLES
  $ mimir item drop MMR-12 3 "Covered by MMR-14"
    with its reason
