mimir unpark · Clear the parked hold. Also takes a seed (KEY-sN). Also takes a delivery (KEY-dN): hold paused → none.

USAGE
  mimir unpark <id> [options]

ARGUMENTS
  id  The task (KEY-seq), the seed (KEY-sN), or the delivery (KEY-dN).

OPTIONS
      --ascii            Print without color, in ASCII glyphs.
  -f, --format <format>  Select the output format, records by default. One of: ids, json, jsonl, records, table.

GLOBAL OPTIONS
  -h, --help      Show this help.
      --no-input  Ask no questions, and take every answer from options.  (default: true)

EXAMPLES
  $ mimir unpark MMR-3
    clear the hold
  $ mimir unpark MMR-s4
    a seed: back to untriaged
