mimir self-update · Replace this application with another release.

USAGE
  mimir self-update [version] [options]

ARGUMENTS
  version  A release version to install, newer or older, such as 1.4.0.

OPTIONS
      --channel <channel>  The release channel to follow, such as stable or next.
      --check              Report what an update would do and replace nothing.

GLOBAL OPTIONS
  -h, --help      Show this help.
      --no-input  Ask no questions, and take every answer from options.  (default: true)
