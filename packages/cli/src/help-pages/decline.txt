mimir decline · Record a review finding a delivery chose not to act on, with its reason.

USAGE
  mimir decline <id> <finding> [words...] [options]

ARGUMENTS
  id       The delivery (KEY-dN).
  finding  The finding, quoted as one argument.
  words    Why it was declined, in place of --reason.

OPTIONS
      --reason <text>    Why it was declined, in place of the words after the finding.
      --ascii            Print without color, in ASCII glyphs.
  -f, --format <format>  Select the output format, records by default. One of: ids, json, jsonl, records, table.

GLOBAL OPTIONS
  -h, --help      Show this help.
      --no-input  Ask no questions, and take every answer from options.  (default: true)

EXAMPLES
  $ mimir decline MMR-d3 "Retry the clone on a fetch failure" "Filed as MMR-s55"
    the finding, then why it was declined
  $ mimir decline MMR-d3 "Retry the clone on a fetch failure" --reason "Filed as MMR-s55"
    the reason as an option
